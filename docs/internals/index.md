# Internals

These pages are about the engine itself rather than about making packs and
mods. They are for people working on Arklay.

- [Architecture](architecture.md): how the engine is put together: the
  pack, the room loop, the renderers, audio, the scripts.
- [Performance](performance.md): the frame-time and load budgets, and how
  they are measured.
- [Status](status.md): what works, milestone by milestone, the format
  coverage and the known gaps.
- [Deviations](deviations/index.md): every place the engine knowingly
  differs from the original games, and why.
