# RE2 deviations

RE2 play is being ported to exactness (Phase C of #244), checked tick by tick against the original with `scripts/oracle-re2.py` (`oracle/re2.md`). Every place where RE2 play knowingly still differs from the original is listed here, so that the rest of the port has the list to work from.

## Player

The pad's walking, running, backing, turning, hurt clips and scripted motions (`player2`, a port of `Player_move`), collision and `Sce_Damage` areas match the original on random walks around room 100 for both characters. On the route, random walks through rooms 101, 11A and 11B, each entered through its door, match too (`walk_101.txt`, `walk_11a.txt` and `walk_11b.txt`, `oracle/re2.md`): `Rnd`, the player, `mot`, the camera, Kendo and every zombie on every tick, with the zombies on and with them dead, through the zombies' grabs and, in room 11B, to the end of the game over. The back walk (`pl_mv03`, `0x4da7c0`) looks at an enemy: unhurt, every 15 ticks, it takes clip 1 while `FUN_004daa60` finds one in play within 5000 units, alive, upright, within 800 of straight ahead, in clear sight head to head and facing the player, and clip 0 when none is, keeping the frame (clip 0's on a multiple of 7). The port backed on clip 0 only, so these walks used to part from the original on the first tick of backing up with a zombie in view. Kendo's `Life` is -1, so he doesn't count. The search's `+0x1B4` (the enemy it found) isn't kept, and the back walk's two bytes at `+0x16A` and `+0x16B` are their own fields in the port, where the original shares them with other routines. The cut of a third of the step that the back walk, the walk and the run make when `Water` (`+0x10C`) is more than 500 below `+0x1C2` isn't ported. Those rooms have no water.

Running was measured on the whole route, each room entered through the previous room's real door (`run_118.txt` to `run_200.txt` and `run_102.txt`, `oracle/re2.md`). Each walk runs in every direction, with diagonals, backing, quick stops and an action press while running, for 2356 ticks after the door (4620 for the chain from room 100 to room 102). Through the doors 102 to 118, 119 to 11A, 11A to 11B, 11B to 103 and 103 to 200, with the run-heavy walk and with a plain random walk, and through 118 to 119 with the plain walk, `room`, `cam`, `p.x`, `p.y`, `p.z`, `p.angle`, `p.beh`, `p.as`, `p.hp` and `mot` match on every tick, including the zombies' hits in 11A and 11B (to the game over in 11B). The chain 100 to 101 to 102 (Kendo's scene, then room 102) matches the same fields for all 4620 ticks apart from `mot` until the first step at 1300, as `door_101_102.txt` does, and `rng` and the zombies match on every tick but the door's. Room 102's zombies used to part from the original at aligned tick 3920, slot 1 taking routine 10 (the wall a script chose) where the original stays in 1: arklay kept an enemy's interaction area (`+0x0A`) from one tick to the next, so room 102's script still saw the zombie in area 4 after it had left and raised `Sce_free0`'s 0x8000. The original clears every enemy's and object's areas to 0xFF after each scheduler pass (`Sce_work_clr_at`), and arklay now does too. Room 119's camera cut matches: the script's `cut_replace` swaps two camera ids in every zone's `cam_from` and `cam_to` (`Cut_replace`; the gates are not touched), so the original cuts from camera 1 to camera 4 at `x` 2254 on `z` -19080, on the same tick as arklay, and in `run_119.txt` every field matches from there through a zombie's hit from aligned tick 757 and the game over that follows. `cut_replace`'s last step, switching to camera `b` when camera `a` is the active one, isn't ported.

- **The held weapon.** Equipping from the inventory or the script's `weapon_chg` changes the hand and the walk's clips (`Pl_weapon_ch`). Of `Plw_init_tbl`'s per-weapon set-up (`0x53A2B8`), the knife's and the shotguns' entries do nothing, and the handguns' and magnums' (`0x4D2FC0`) set up the magazine part, which is ported; its other step, which sets weapon 2's frame row (`0x53A30B`) to 3 for player ids 8 and 0xA, isn't, nor are the other weapons' entries or `Weapon_cls`. EQUIP of the knife and of the shotgun from Leon's status screen in room 102 (`weapon_knife`, `weapon_knife_wall` and `weapon_shotgun`, `oracle/re2.md`, "Weapons") matches every compared field on every tick, `rseed` and `snd` included.
- **The weapons' stance.** `Pl_move`'s routine 5 is `pl_mv05_w1` for Leon's and Claire's starting handguns, the magnums and the shotguns: raising, tilting, firing, reloading and lowering, with each weapon's timing, foot, kick, ammunition, flash, smoke, shell and shot rectangles. In a tick with both, the shotgun's pump (its frame's sound) plays before the falling shell's (`Esp_`), as in the original, where the effects run after `Player_move`. The knife's is `pl_mv05_w0`: the raise (with `Em_ck`'s reach at 5000, or 30000 in rooms 40A and 704), the slash at each tilt, its frame sounds, the let-go that ends it after frame 20, and the bounce (routine 6, clip 0x10, sound 0) when `Sca_ck_hit` finds a wall within 10 of the blade's tip in frames 7 to 12. Its body search and the hit (`0x4D7E30`, with sound 0x1060001 and the blood), its water splashes, id 0xD's sparks and `Sca_ck_hit`'s object models aren't ported; the search finds nothing in a room with no enemies. The custom handgun, the Colt, the bowgun, the grenade launchers and the heavier weapons don't raise. Firing is the original's, with `Shoot_camera`'s sparks when the gun lines up with the camera (checked by moving the camera on to the gun's line in both, since a real cut hardly ever lines up).
- **What a magnum or shotgun does to a zombie's body is only partly drawn.** `Em10Dm05`, `Em10Dm02` and their deaths run as the original does, a thrown arm falls and bleeds as `Parts_down` has it, and an arm the shove pulls off (`FUN_0045bd20`) follows the player's hands and comes away as it does in the original. A thrown part's wall check passes its last position as `Sca_ck_info`'s previous one, which wasn't compared against the original. The water branches and `Routine_1` 9 and 0xC paths of these reactions aren't ported.
- **What a climbing licker doesn't do.** `Em_22` climbs walls and ceilings and drops from them as the original does, through `Super_matrix` and `Sca_ck_em`'s surface checks. Not yet checked against the original: the wounded climb (`Routine_1` 0x15), `Em22_Damage` 2 and 5 and `Em22_Die` 2 to 4 on a wall or ceiling, the scripted routines 0x10, 0x12, 0x16 and 0x17, and room 215. `Em22_before`'s fade of a dying licker's shadow (`+0x21b`) isn't ported, so the shadow stays until the body goes. `Sca_hit`'s walls skip the room's object models.
- **The second licker.** `Em_24` runs `Em_22`'s code (`lib/licker.lua`) with its own clips, life, starts, choices, damage table and the last row of its blood table. It doesn't climb, and its scripted drop only waits for `Sce_free0`. - **What a dog doesn't show.** `Em_20` matches the original on `Rnd`, position, routine, motion and life, including its take-down of the player. The dog keeps the destination it had, so its path can differ from the original's until it next looks. This showed in one fight of 16, after about 1370 ticks.
- **What a crow doesn't show.** `Em_21` matches the original on `Rnd`, position, routine, motion and life, including its pecks, its hold on the player, scenario B's perched crows and the bursts of a magnum. `Parts_bomb`'s parts move as the original's do but are drawn as ordinary parts, not through `Bomb_Parts_SortGT`. `hard_mode` is taken as off (life 10, peck 5). ## Collision

- **Other bodies' floors.** The player's `Floor` (`+0x106`) is ported (#377): `Sca_ck_em` checks it, and the slope, stairs, climb and jump-down routines update it. Other bodies still take their floor from their height, where the original keeps it in `nFloor`, which rounds differently on a slope (`Sca_hit_slope`).
- **Script objects.** `obj_model_set` places and draws a room's models, and the scripts can move them through `work_set`. They hold the player out of their boxes as `Om_move` ends (`Oma_ck_oba`), but they don't hold enemies out, and they can't be pushed or pulled (`Oma_ck_oba`'s other modes, `Oma_pull`, `Om_move_box`). The plant pots in room 116 are placed with attribute 0x10, so they draw unlit and neither light space touches them. On cut 0 the original's are about 1.08 times as bright as arklay's, a box of about 27 by 14 pixels. Not ported: water (`Mizu_trans`), colour changes (`Cc_work`) and an object parented to an enemy. `Om_disp`'s attribute 0x10 draws an object unlit and attribute 2 blends it by half. ## Effects

- **Which effects move.** `Esp_call`'s behaviours 0 to 4, 6, 0xB, 0xC and 0xD are ported, which covers room 100's fire and smoke. The others only play their frames in place and log once.
- **3D effects.** `Esp_trans_3d`'s sprites draw at the turn their script gave them, from `sce_espr3d_on` and `sce_espr3d_on2`. On a body's or a work's matrix they take its position but not its turn, and `Mirror_in_view`'s second pass isn't drawn.
- **Camera shake.** `sce_shake_on` moves the frame as `SetGeomOffset` does. No campaign room runs it without a trigger, so no parity run has seen it. `Scr_quake`'s own table isn't ported.
- **The frames of a cut.** On the tick a cut changes and the tick after, the original shows its last frame again; arklay draws the old cut on both ticks with that tick's models, so anything moving has moved on by a tick or two. Room 200's opening pan changes cut on two consecutive ticks (11, then 0, then 10), and on the first of them its script also scrolls the pan back to the top; the original keeps showing cut 11's last frame, at its old scroll, until the new cut's first frame. arklay now does the same, keeping the drawn cut and its scroll while a change follows a change. A cut that changes and changes back two ticks later isn't held: on the fought route the camera goes from cut 4 to 5 on tick 4435 and back to 4 on 4437, and the original shows cut 5 on 4437 and 4438 where arklay shows cut 4 (a mean error of 40 on those two frames). From a warp into room 200, every frame from tick 14 to 375 is within a mean error of 0.25 of the original's (0.19 over all 376). Ticks 0 to 13 differ (6.5 down to 0.8): the original is black on ticks 0 and 1 and has its letterbox bars fully in by its first frame, because its scene started during the warp, while arklay's `--room` start fades in and slides the bars in.
- **Script fades.** `sce_fade_set` and `sce_fade_adjust` run all four fades, over the whole screen and only in black or adding modes. Mode 3, used once, adds, and the colour mask, which the campaign always leaves at 7, is ignored. `Sce_fade_adjust`'s hold in stage 6's lit rooms isn't ported.
- **Attached effects.** An effect on a body's matrix is placed as if on the world's.
- **The sheets.** A room's effects use the low-resolution TIMs in its RDT, not `espdat1` and `espdat2`, and the shared ones `espcore.bin`'s six low-resolution TIMs (`Graphicsprdata == 0`), not `tex216.adt` and `tex316.adt`.
- **Randomness.** Effects draw from their own stream, not `Rnd`.
- **Collisions and pauses.** Effects don't hit water or SCA records, and `fg_stop` doesn't stop them. In RE2 rooms, `Esp_05` checks `Water_ck`: what falls below a water area's level goes there, in a splash (`Esp_call3` kind 0x1a, row 1, of its size), so a spent case in room 302 doesn't bounce.
- **Smoothing.** Effect quads aren't tweened at high frame rates.

## Game flow

- **Debug jumps.** The player arrives through the door back from a neighbour room. With no such door the player stands in the room's own first door, and with no door at all under the first camera. The cut is the first of the door's, the zone's and the rest that shows the player. The original has no such jump. In rooms 200 and 500 no cut shows the placement.

- **Pick-ups.** `Sce_Item` opens the get-item window (`0x4FD560`), which holds the world from the next tick, as the original's game mode 2 does: the question shows on the seventh tick, a yes takes the item 20 ticks after the answer with "You've taken the ...", a no counts out 15 ticks, a full inventory says "You can't carry any more items.", and play comes back 11 ticks after the last message closes, with the player's motion restarted.
