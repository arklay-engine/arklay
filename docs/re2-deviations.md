# RE2 deviations

RE2 play is being ported to exactness (Phase C of #244), checked tick by tick against the original with `scripts/oracle-re2.py` (`oracle/re2.md`). Every place where RE2 play knowingly still differs from the original is listed here, so that the rest of the port has the list to work from.

## Player

The pad's walking, running, backing, turning, hurt clips and scripted motions (`player2`, a port of `Player_move`), collision and `Sce_Damage` areas match the original on random walks around room 100 for both characters.

- **The held weapon.** Equipping from the inventory or the script's `weapon_chg` changes the hand and the walk's clips (`Pl_weapon_ch`), but `Plw_init_tbl`'s per-weapon set-up and `Weapon_cls` aren't ported.
- **The guns' stance only.** `Pl_move`'s routine 5 is `pl_mv05_w1` for Leon's and Claire's starting handguns, the magnums and the shotguns: raising, tilting, firing, reloading and lowering, with each weapon's timing, foot, kick, ammunition, flash, smoke, shell and shot rectangles. The knife, the custom handgun, the Colt, the bowgun, the grenade launchers and the heavier weapons don't raise. Firing is the original's, with `Shoot_camera`'s sparks when the gun lines up with the camera (checked by moving the camera on to the gun's line in both, since a real cut hardly ever lines up).
- **What a magnum or shotgun does to a zombie's body is only partly drawn.** `Em10Dm05`, `Em10Dm02` and their deaths run as the original does, a thrown arm falls and bleeds as `Parts_down` has it, and an arm the shove pulls off (`FUN_0045bd20`) follows the player's hands and comes away as it does in the original. Parts that `Be_flg` 0x4A blows apart (`Parts_bomb`) are just hidden. These aren't drawn: `Em10Dm02`'s body tilt, the torn body's and the stump's mesh swaps, the scaling of what's left where a part came off, and a thrown part's own lighting. `Esp_kill` doesn't put out the flames on a lost part. A thrown part's wall check passes its last position as `Sca_ck_info`'s previous one, which wasn't compared against the original. The water branches and `Routine_1` 9 and 0xC paths of these reactions aren't ported.
- **What a climbing licker doesn't do.** `Em_22` climbs walls and ceilings and drops from them as the original does, through `Super_matrix` and `Sca_ck_em`'s surface checks. Not yet checked against the original: the wounded climb (`Routine_1` 0x15), `Em22_Damage` 2 and 5 and `Em22_Die` 2 to 4 on a wall or ceiling, the scripted routines 0x10, 0x12, 0x16 and 0x17, and room 215. `Em22_before`'s fade of a dying licker's shadow (`+0x21b`) isn't ported, so the shadow stays until the body goes. `Sca_hit`'s walls skip the room's object models.
- **The second licker.** `Em_24` runs `Em_22`'s code (`lib/licker.lua`) with its own clips, life, starts, choices, damage table and the last row of its blood table. It doesn't climb, and its scripted drop only waits for `Sce_free0`. Its kill bite (`FUN_0046efe0`) isn't ported, and the flag 0x8000 its death puts on every part (`FUN_0046ed90`, for the mirror's view) isn't kept. Its tongue has its own owner (`DWORD_00689538`) in the original but shares the licker's in arklay, which only matters with both kinds in one room. Its first pose takes `Joint_move`'s blend through the same call as `Joint_move2`'s. The `Em_24` damage case 2 (`FUN_0046e350`) is never picked, so it isn't ported.
- **A licker with no route.** In room 20E, `kara_rootck2` finds no route and writes the destination from its uninitialised stack, which alternates in the original. Arklay keeps the destination at zero. A wall licker there matches the original for about 600 ticks, then strays.
- **What a dog doesn't show.** `Em_20` matches the original on `Rnd`, position, routine, motion and life, including its take-down of the player. A blast's parts (`Parts_bomb`) show as blood, not as the original's pieces, so a dead dog keeps the parts the original throws. Its burning colours and the fade of its shadow (`em20_die_2`) aren't drawn, and `Die_move_set_cut`'s camera isn't ported. A partner isn't a target, and the original's blood censor flag isn't read. Its head's turning rates are the zombie's.
- **A dog off the guide areas.** Heading for a guide area from outside all of them, `Root_ck` calls `kara_rootck2` with area 0xFF. That reads a row past the table, finds no route and writes the destination from its uninitialised stack. The dog keeps the destination it had, so its path can differ from the original's until it next looks. This showed in one fight of 16, after about 1370 ticks.
- **What a crow doesn't show.** `Em_21` matches the original on `Rnd`, position, routine, motion and life, including its pecks, its hold on the player, scenario B's perched crows and the bursts of a magnum. `Parts_bomb`'s parts move as the original's do but are drawn as ordinary parts, not through `Bomb_Parts_SortGT`. `hard_mode` is taken as off (life 10, peck 5). When the player is dead, a crow off the guide areas steers as the dog's note below describes; that showed after about 1450 ticks in one run, with `Rnd` still matching.
- **What Mr. X doesn't show.** `Em_2A` (`enemy/2a/enemy.lua`) matches the original on `Rnd`, the player, position, routine, motion and life in rooms 20C, 20D, 109 and 10C: his walk and stride, the swings, the shove, the overhead blow, the charge, the kill by the neck, his flinch and his drop to one knee, with weapons 2, 5 and 7. Its damage rows for weapons 3, 4, 6 and 8 come from the decompilation, but no run shot him with those weapons, so they are unchecked. In rooms 117, 507, 602, 604, 612 and 30A his scene states (`Routine_1` 9 to 14 and 17) were checked only while they wait for their script; their scripted steps weren't reached. Nothing in the original's look picks the backhand (`Routine_1` 7), so it wasn't run. The flinch from behind (`Damage_flg` 0x20) wasn't reached. `Routine_0` 3 is never entered, because he goes down on one knee below 20000 life and the dead flag is raised then, so it does nothing. The blood that keeps coming over the ticks after a hit with weapons 0xA, 0xE, 0xF and 0x12 (`+0x220`'s callbacks) isn't ported, so those weapons draw no blood on him. `Routine_1` 0x11's `Attribute |= 0x80` isn't kept. `hard_mode` is taken as off.
- **What Mr. X's slot held before.** Neither `MrX_init` nor `Sce_em_set` clears `+0x230` and `+0x1d2`, so the original's one-hit-in-four flinch count and last reaction start with whatever the slot's last body left there. arklay starts the count past 0x20, as it was after the street in the run checked, so the first hit draws a new order as the original's does, and it starts the reaction at 0. With another previous value, the original's first hits flinch at different times, and its first hit's blood comes from a different third of the table.
- **What a zombie's slot held before.** The walk and the stand's wander turn toward `Dest` (`+0x1c4`, `+0x1c6`), but neither `Sce_em_set` nor `Em10_Init` sets it, and `Root_ck` leaves it alone while the zombie or the player is off the guide areas. The original's zombie then walks toward whatever the memory under its work held from the previous room. In room 110, entered from the street with `--warp 110:-17500:-7200:-9500:0 --life 200` and no input, the three zombies' works hold 8675, 4355; 21108, 30720; and 2115, 2115, which don't change in 1000 ticks. arklay starts `Dest` at 0, so its zombies turn toward the room's origin instead: zombie 2 parts from the original at tick 112, sliding along a wall, and zombie 1 at tick 603. Under `--room` there's a second difference: the room load's `Root_ck` runs before the setup script places the player, so it routes toward a player at 0, 0, 0 and writes a `Dest` the original never writes. With both set by hand to the original's, `Rnd`, the player and the zombies match for the 1000 ticks run. Neither is a difference in the turn itself.
- **Rooms 109 and 10C.** They place Mr. X only with `fg_status` bit 6 (`ck 1,6`). `Title_game_init` and the new game's setup (`FUN_004b73c0`) both set it, but only when `fg_system` has 8 or 1 set, which only the title menu's extra modes do (`fg_system |= 9`); a load restores whatever the save had. arklay has no extra modes, so he shows there only with that flag set by hand. Room 10D's Mr. X also wants `cmp(0, 27, 0, 270)`, which no run reached.
- **A crow's feathers late in a fight.** In room 109 with `crow_held.txt` (`oracle/re2.md`), the player and the crows match the original through the first peck, the release straight into aiming and the turn after it. At tick 787 one of the draws of `Rnd` that the feathers' swing and bounce (`esp2`) make comes a tick later than the original's, and the stream is back in step on the next tick. At the second peck (tick 811) arklay draws three fewer, so `Rnd`, and then the crows and the player, differ from there. Both are in the effects, not the player's code.
- **A push at the start of a scripted motion or a hit.** In room 216, in two runs, the original moved the player about 100 units on the tick a `plc_motion` or a dog's hit began, when the player had stopped against something; arklay doesn't. The cause isn't known; the player's position then stays offset by that much.
- **What a licker doesn't show.** `Em22_Pldm` and `fg_system`'s decapitation aren't ported, so its killing bite keeps the original's timing and random stream but shows a plain fall. `Die_move_set_cut`'s camera for a held death isn't ported for any killer. Its dying and burning colours (`FUN_0046ed90`, `FUN_0046b840`) aren't drawn. A partner isn't a target. At some angles a claw (part 6) shows a small gap, so a finger looks detached; the cause isn't known.
- **The dropped magazine.** `Mag_set` and `Mag_down` match, but the part's place under the hand was only read from the original for Leon's weapons 2 and 5, and it's assumed for the rest. Claire's isn't checked.
- **The pose before play.** After a door the first tick's blend starts from the skeleton's rest, every joint's turn zeroed, as the original's does, and the frame before it draws the head there. At the start of a game it still starts from the clip's own frame rather than the pose `Pl_init` leaves, so raising the gun within the first few ticks of a game lands a few units off.
- **Stairs.** `pl_mv_07` climbs and descends them, and the shadow follows the steps: `Game_loop` probes `Sca_ck_hit` at part 0's world place plus `Kage_ofs`, which `pl_mv_07` grows to 200 on the stairs (#381). The player's `Floor` (`+0x106`) is the original's (#377), and `Sca_ck_em` checks against it; the writers the original also has (`sce_em_set`, `member_set2` and some enemy routines) aren't modelled.
- **Falls and death.** `Pl_damage`'s falls (`Routine_1` 4 and 5) play as the plain hit, without their clips or getting up: those clips are `pSub1`'s, which only an enemy's grab sets, and no enemy that uses them is ported. `Pl_die` falls and cries as the original does, checked by forcing the routine in both; `Pl_dead` doesn't grow the shadow into its pool of blood, which `Die_vcut_set` culls anyway. `Pl_dead`'s own game over runs `Die` from falling below zero, not from `Pl_life_down`'s flag.
- **What the player's routine leaves out.** Of water, only `Water` itself and the footsteps' ripples are ported ("Footsteps"). The rest of what the routines do in water isn't: the walk's, run's, back step's and turn's splashes from the hands (parts 11 and 14, from `Water` 300 under them, each drawing `Rnd`), their slowdown once `Water` is 500 over `Ground`, and `Pl_water` in `pl_mv_08` and in the stances other than `pl_mv05_w1`. Room 302's water (-180) is too shallow for the walk's, run's, back step's and turn's hand splashes. In `pl_mv05_w1_t0` and `_t2` (the raise and the shot), `Pl_water` and the hands' splashes are ported: on `Move_cnt` 1, 3 and 5, while `Water` is above `Ground`, four rings (`Esp_call` kind 0x1a, row 0) at part 0's place at the water's level, each turned by `Rnd` and sized by another `Rnd` (0x818 for the first two, 0xC00 for the others), and a splash off part 11 or 14 when the water is within 300 of it (200 for part 14 in the shot). They read the parts as the last tick left them (`Workm`), also in the shot, where `Joint_move` runs before them: room 405's water (-1040) is deep enough that the shotgun's fire clip takes part 14 across that line, and posing it on the new frame drew the splash a tick early. `_t3`'s hand splashes (the lowering) aren't ported. In the shot they come before `pl_wp02_tbl`'s flash and case, as in the original, since the effects take their `Rnd` in the order they were called. `Lim_dir_ck`'s clip swap isn't ported either. The neck follows `plc_neck` and `Pl_neck`'s aim at the nearest enemy, matching the original's flags and whether it has a target through all of `street_shoot.txt`. Its turn is usually within a few units of 4096, as far as 47 when the target's head moves fast, because the port places a head from the pose where the original uses its last drawn matrix. Only bodies arklay models can be looked at.
- **Climbing.** `Oma_pl_updown_ck` reads only the room's SCA records, not object models, and treats every shape as its box.

- **The game over.** `Die`'s timing, stops, music cut, white-out and `YOU DIED` text (`Die_spr_*`, with its `Rnd` draws) are the original's, on the room hidden as `Die_vcut_set` and `Bg_set_mode` hide it, and the game returns to the title. This is the original's behaviour with `censorship_off` clear. With it set the original shows the room's models from `Die_move_set_cut`'s spiralling camera under `Die_light_set`, with `Die_blood_move_main`'s blood; neither is ported. The text's corners are drawn as triangles without the original's depth order, and `Die_sprt_load`'s check for an ally's death isn't ported. `Game_loop`'s checks before starting `Die` (no other task running, the player's cut) are skipped.

## Collision

- **Other bodies' floors.** The player's `Floor` (`+0x106`) is ported (#377): `Sca_ck_em` checks it, and the slope, stairs, climb and jump-down routines update it. Other bodies still take their floor from their height, where the original keeps it in `nFloor`, which rounds differently on a slope (`Sca_hit_slope`).
- **Script objects.** `obj_model_set` places and draws a room's models, and the scripts can move them through `work_set`. They hold the player out of their boxes as `Om_move` ends (`Oma_ck_oba`), but they don't hold enemies out, and they can't be pushed or pulled (`Oma_ck_oba`'s other modes, `Oma_pull`, `Om_move_box`). The plant pots in room 116 are placed with attribute 0x10, so they draw unlit and neither light space touches them. On cut 0 the original's are about 1.08 times as bright as arklay's, a box of about 27 by 14 pixels. Not ported: water (`Mizu_trans`), colour changes (`Cc_work`) and an object parented to an enemy. `Om_disp`'s attribute 0x10 draws an object unlit and attribute 2 blends it by half. A blended object draws full bright even without attribute 0x10, which the original lights (nine campaign objects). Blended objects don't get its nearer sort key or the different blend of rooms 302 and 30A, and `Om_disp`'s per-model modes aren't ported. Room 60D's floor mist is brighter in the original than here, and the cause isn't known.
- **Objects and other bodies.** Only the room's SCA records block. `Sca_ck_hit`'s object models (`Omd_in_check`) and `Oba_ck_em2`'s other bodies don't.
- **Offsets.** `Sca_hit_*` add `Atd`'s `Ofs_x` and `Ofs_z`, which are zero for the player. Other bodies will need them.

## Doors

- **The reach and auto probes.** The bits of an area's `sat` byte are reduced to RE1's action-key, position and reach probes. The other triggers (the low nibble's non-player bits) are ignored.
- **Locked doors.** The key messages are the executable's wording, written from memory (`games/re2/language/en-US.txt`), and the door plays no lock sound.
- **The door scripts' other inputs.** The knock type isn't used.
- **Door timing.** Every door on the route from room 100 to room 200 (100 to 101, 101 to 102, 102 to 118, 118 to 119, 119 to 11A, 11A to 11B, 11B to 103 and 103 to 200) switches rooms on the original's tick, with and without sound, and matches it for 200 ticks and more after the switch on `Rnd`, the player, the head, the camera, the stage and every enemy's position and facing, except for 118 to 119 and 103 to 200 (below). The old room's enemies go on the placement tick and the new room loads a tick later; across a stage change they go a tick later and it loads three ticks after the placement. 101 to 102 is traced from room 100's door through Kendo's scene (`door_101_102.txt`, `oracle/re2.md`): both doors switch on the original's tick, and `Rnd`, the player, the camera, Kendo and the zombies match on every tick to the end of room 102's first 400, apart from each door's own tick. The music's state there is a tick behind the original's for three ticks at each room load. 118 to 119 was traced from a warp whose first tick already differs on `Rnd`, so room 119's zombies arrive where the original's do but with other lives, which come from `Rnd`. 103 to 200 was too: room 103's zombies differ from the warp's first tick, so `Rnd` differs throughout, though the door switches on the original's tick. Other doors haven't been traced, and `Door_load`'s own sleeps depend on the door's sound bank.

## Scripts

- **Sleeps in an init procedure.** They don't wait: a room's init runs to its end inside the load.
- **Script characters.** The characters `_Spl` runs (ids 0x40 to 0x5B, from `sce_em_set`) take `work_set 3`, move on `Spl_Scenario` with their own speeds, turns and banks and stop on the records of `Sca_ck_em`'s mask, and room 101's scene with Kendo matches the original tick for tick. Marvin's arms draw the meshes his init (`0x4f00e0`) chooses by `Type`, with the gun in his hand at `0x800`; the fade between his two sets of arms (`Type`'s `0x200`, `0x4f0550`) runs its 127 ticks and ends with the zombie's arms, but the glow it puts on them (`Poly_rgb`) isn't drawn. A `member_set` of a character's `Id` (member 6) to a zombie's turns it into one that keeps its model, as Marvin does in room 202 with `4:7` and `4:9` set. They have no `Spl_Auto` yet, so a character outside a script's `plc_*` stands still, and no `Oba_ck_em` or footsteps of their own. `parts_set` only does its case 2, a part's `Be_flg`, which hides and shows it: its cases for a part's colour, attribute and rotation, in rooms 106 and 112's loops, do nothing. A `plc_neck` aimed at someone not modelled, such as a zombie, leaves the head where it was. Their `Spd` isn't compared: `Spl_Init`'s `Joint_move` leaves values in it that nothing reads before `Spl_sce*` sets them. `splayer_work` (`sce_em_set` slot 255, `work_set 2`) is one of them, with its own slot and stop bit: Sherry's scene in room 114 matches too. The enemies other than zombies aren't modelled: their `plc_dest` arrives at once, `member_cmp` on one always answers false and any `member_set` of one is dropped, so a script waiting on one's motion moves on. `tests/real/re2_real.rs` runs every main procedure and names those still holding (113, none in the rooms from the start to the police station).
- **Scripted zombies and body parts.** Routine 8 (`Em10_Scene`) stands, walks, grabs and eats as room 101's scene asks, and `Spl_Sce20` plays Kendo's end. `FUN_0045a240`, its case 4, isn't ported, since no script asks for it. Of `Spl_Sce20`'s `FUN_004f6800`, the parts' `Free` and colour effects and the small vertical bob aren't ported, and the player's height isn't traced there. `sce_parts_bomb` hides the part and flags it but draws no burst of `Parts_bomb`. `Get_matrix` covers identity and the player and enemy works, not objects, so an effect a script places on an object starts at its position alone.
- **Zombies.** `Em_Zombie` runs ids 0x10 to 0x1F: `Em10_Init`, standing, wandering, chasing, lying and getting up, on `Root_ck`'s line of sight and guide areas, sharing `Rnd` with the effects. Six zombies left alone in room 100 match the original tick for tick until the first one reaches the player, apart from one angle on the first tick, because the original's player has already been pushed out of the wall where arklay's hasn't. They push the player (`Oba_ck_em2`), grab, bite and are shoved off, and a kill runs the player's death in their hands, their letting the body down and the others coming over to eat it, all matching the original through 1,997 ticks of `street_shoot.txt`, the end of the game over. `street_wander.txt` over 1,500 ticks and `street_aim.txt` over 800, where zombies grab and let go of the player at low life, match to the end too. In room 110 they part from the original only through `Dest`'s starting value ("What a zombie's slot held before"). In rooms 610 and 11B under `--room` they leave the stand two ticks after the original's, because the player's neck has no position on arklay's first two ticks, so the first sight checks fail (`oracle/re2.md`). That is the `--room` load, not the zombie's code: with the neck in place from the load, both rooms match over 1056 ticks. A real door into room 11B, from room 11A, matches the original from the door through the room's first 393 ticks of play, with either of the room's sets of zombies. A lunging zombie (state 0xC) reaches for the player in the lunge's last step, `Routine_2` of 3, as `FUN_00454f30` does; the port used to test the clip number there, so a lunge never took hold. `first_door.txt` from a new game with the zombies on used to part from the original at tick 370, where the original's zombie 4 grabs the player out of its lunge. It now matches on `Rnd`, the player and all six zombies to the end of the game over at tick 1459, after which the original returns to the title, apart from zombie 1's angle on the first tick. Room 103's script turns a zombie toward a wall by where it stands. `Sce_at_check` runs an enemy pass (mask 2) after the player's, which puts the last area an enemy was in at `+0xB`, script member 9, and in the script's area variable. 0xFF means it hasn't been in one. When zombie 1 is in area 3 and `Sce_rnd` reaches 32000, the room's loop sets its `Sce_free0` to `0x8C00`. The walk's and the chase's wall check (`FUN_00452930`, `FUN_00453010`) read that word's high byte at `+0x1D5`, where the port read `Sce_free1`. Room 11B's door to room 103 (`door_11b.txt` from `--warp 11B:11393:0:-15351:3072`, `oracle/re2.md`) used to part from the original at tick 884, where the original's zombie 1 turns to the wall and arklay's didn't. Of the area handlers, the enemy pass runs none: on the route only kind 3, whose handler does nothing, has the enemy bit, but the sewers' and the lab's water areas (kind 7), room 615's flag area (kind 6) and a door in room 410 have it too. arklay also doesn't put the two area variables back to -1 after each tick's scripts, as the original's scheduler does, for the player's areas or an enemy's. These two changes leave every other zombie recipe's trace identical: the street set, the twelve gun fights, `door_101_102.txt`, `kendo_attacked.txt`, the doors into rooms 102, 118, 119, 11A and 11B (`door_11b.txt` with both sets of zombies), and rooms 610 and 11B under `--room`. ## Effects

- **Which effects move.** `Esp_call`'s behaviours 0 to 4, 6, 0xB, 0xC and 0xD are ported, which covers room 100's fire and smoke. The others only play their frames in place and log once.
- **3D effects.** `Esp_trans_3d`'s sprites draw at the turn their script gave them, from `sce_espr3d_on` and `sce_espr3d_on2`. On a body's or a work's matrix they take its position but not its turn, and `Mirror_in_view`'s second pass isn't drawn.
- **Camera shake.** `sce_shake_on` moves the frame as `SetGeomOffset` does. No campaign room runs it without a trigger, so no parity run has seen it. `Scr_quake`'s own table isn't ported.
- **The frames of a cut.** On the tick a cut changes and the tick after, the original shows its last frame again; arklay draws the old cut on both ticks with that tick's models, so anything moving has moved on by a tick or two. Room 200's opening pan changes cut on two consecutive ticks (11, then 0, then 10), and on the first of them its script also scrolls the pan back to the top; the original keeps showing cut 11's last frame, at its old scroll, until the new cut's first frame. arklay now does the same, keeping the drawn cut and its scroll while a change follows a change. From a warp into room 200, every frame from tick 14 to 375 is within a mean error of 0.25 of the original's (0.19 over all 376). Ticks 0 to 13 differ (6.5 down to 0.8): the original is black on ticks 0 and 1 and has its letterbox bars fully in by its first frame, because its scene started during the warp, while arklay's `--room` start fades in and slides the bars in.
- **Script fades.** `sce_fade_set` and `sce_fade_adjust` run all four fades, over the whole screen and only in black or adding modes. Mode 3, used once, adds, and the colour mask, which the campaign always leaves at 7, is ignored. `Sce_fade_adjust`'s hold in stage 6's lit rooms isn't ported.
- **Attached effects.** An effect on a body's matrix is placed as if on the world's.
- **The sheets.** A room's effects use the low-resolution TIMs in its RDT, not `espdat1` and `espdat2`, and the shared ones `espcore.bin`'s six low-resolution TIMs (`Graphicsprdata == 0`), not `tex216.adt` and `tex316.adt`.
- **Randomness.** Effects draw from their own stream, not `Rnd`.
- **Collisions and pauses.** Effects don't hit water or SCA records, and `fg_stop` doesn't stop them. In RE2 rooms, `Esp_05` checks `Water_ck`: what falls below a water area's level goes there, in a splash (`Esp_call3` kind 0x1a, row 1, of its size), so a spent case in room 302 doesn't bounce.
- **Smoothing.** Effect quads aren't tweened at high frame rates.

## Game flow

- **Debug jumps.** The player arrives through the door back from a neighbour room. With no such door the player stands in the room's own first door, and with no door at all under the first camera. The cut is the first of the door's, the zone's and the rest that shows the player. The original has no such jump. In rooms 200 and 500 no cut shows the placement.

- **Pick-ups.** The item goes straight into the inventory with a "You picked up" message. There is no pick-up screen or item model.
- **Messages.** The ones the executable draws itself are written from memory, and every message uses RE1's Western font. A room's text is its RDT's English list (`rdt + 0x40`), where the executable reads the list before it (`rdt + 0x3C`), so a line with text reveals at the English length; a voiced line has none in either list, so its timing is the original's. `xa_on` plays its line and `wsleep` waits for it, for as long as the line plays with sound, and for `Xa_control`'s count without (half that), so a trace matches the reference, which runs without sound.
- **Start items.** `games/re2/game/new_game.lua` copies `Stage_init_item`'s table for Leon and Claire, with the lighter or lockpick in slot 2 rather than slot 10, and no other character's or mode's row.
- **Text screens.** The title, new-game choice, inventory and item box are plain text stand-ins (`games/re2/ui`), and the save screen has no background: RE2's own art is not converted.
- **The inventory.** Confirm equips a weapon or uses a healing item. There is no CHECK, MOVE, combining, map or file tab, and equipping fires only the handguns, magnums and shotguns.
- **Healing.** The amounts and the cap of 200 are the original's, measured (`item_definitions`): a green herb heals 50, two greens 100, green with blue 50, two greens with blue 100, and three greens and the first aid spray heal to the maximum. Green with red, and green with red and blue, are taken to heal to the maximum too: both reached 200 from 1 life, so only a cap could tell that from a fixed amount, and that was not tested. Not measured in RE2: the blue herb's poison cure (RE1's rule), and whether a herb used at full life is used up.
- **Item definitions.** Only weapons, ammo and healing items have a kind; everything else can't be used from the inventory. Key items work from the door or script that wants them.
- **Save labels.** The save screen names the room by its number, not its place.
- **Item stacks.** Ammo stacks to 255 and stacks into any stack of its own kind.
